Theorem 1

5 required APIs

The following properties hold for NPMLE:

  1. (Existence) The minimizer of the optimization problem (1.1) exists.
  2. (Optimality condition) A distribution ρ̂∈𝒫(ℝd)\widehat\rho\in\mathcal P(\mathbb R^d) is an NPMLE if and only if (i) δℓN(ρ̂)(x)≥−1\delta\ell_N(\widehat\rho)(x)\geq-1 holds for all x∈ℝdx\in\mathbb R^d, and (ii) δℓN(ρ̂)(x)=−1\delta\ell_N(\widehat\rho)(x)=-1 for ρ̂\widehat\rho-a.e. xx.
