For the identifiability of 𝛃*\boldsymbol\beta^*, we assume the following conditions about the distribution of XX, 𝐙\boldsymbol Z and YY hold for the entire paper:

  1. The median of the noise conditional on XX and 𝐙\boldsymbol Z is 00, that is, median⁡(ϵ∣X,𝐙)=0\operatorname{median}(\epsilon\mid X,\boldsymbol Z)=0.

  2. The support of (X,𝐙)(X,\boldsymbol Z) is not contained in any proper linear subspace of ℝp+1\mathbb R^{p+1}.

  4. The distribution of XX conditional on 𝐙\boldsymbol Z has positive density almost everywhere.
