Fundamental Limits of Low-Rank Matrix Estimation with Diverging Aspect Ratios

Introduction — fixed independent factor distributions

In this paper, we assume a stylized model whereby the rows 𝚲,𝚯\boldsymbol\Lambda,\boldsymbol\Theta are mutually independent (and independent of 𝐙\boldsymbol Z) with (𝚲i)i≤n∼iidμΛ(\boldsymbol\Lambda_i)_{i\le n}\overset{\mathrm{iid}}\sim\mu_\Lambda and (𝚯j)j≤d∼iidμΘ(\boldsymbol\Theta_j)_{j\le d}\overset{\mathrm{iid}}\sim\mu_\Theta. Here, μΛ\mu_\Lambda and μΘ\mu_\Theta are fixed probability distributions on ℝr\mathbb R^r. We will be concerned with the problem of determining the Bayes optimal estimation error under the idealized setting in which the distributions μΛ,μΘ\mu_\Lambda,\mu_\Theta and the signal-to-noise ratio sns_n are known to the statistician.
