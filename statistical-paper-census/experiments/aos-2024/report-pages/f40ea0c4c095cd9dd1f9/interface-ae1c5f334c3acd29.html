Section 6.1 — Non-Gaussian Component Analysis, equations (14a) and (14b)

In the Non-Gaussian Component Analysis (NGCA) problem, one seeks to estimate an unknown vector 𝐕∈ℝd\boldsymbol V \in \mathbb R^d with ∥𝐕∥=d\|\boldsymbol V\| = \sqrt{d} from an i.i.d. sample 𝐱1:N\boldsymbol x_{1:N} generated as follows:

𝐱i=ηi1d𝐕+(𝐈d−1d𝐕𝐕𝖳)𝐳i, \boldsymbol x_i = \eta_i \frac1{\sqrt{d}} \boldsymbol V + \left( \boldsymbol I_d- \frac1{d} \boldsymbol V \boldsymbol V^{\scriptscriptstyle{\mathsf{T}}}\right) \boldsymbol z_i, \; (14a) where ηi∈ℝ\eta_i \in \mathbb R and 𝐳i∈ℝd\boldsymbol z_i \in \mathbb R^d are independent random variables with distributions 𝐳i∼𝒩(𝟎,𝐈d),ηi∼ν. \boldsymbol z_i \sim \mathcal{N}\left( \boldsymbol 0,\boldsymbol I_d \right), \quad \eta_i \sim \nu. (14b)

In the above display, ν\nu is a non-Gaussian distribution on ℝ\mathbb R. Let μ𝐕\mu_{\boldsymbol V} denote the distribution of 𝐱i\boldsymbol x_i described by the above generating process (14).
