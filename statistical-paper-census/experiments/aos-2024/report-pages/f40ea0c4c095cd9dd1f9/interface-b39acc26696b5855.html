Notation 2 — Finite measures and barycenter

All measures we consider will be finite and have a finite first moment, i.e., ∫|x|μ(dx)<∞\int|x|\mu(dx)<\infty. For a Polish space 𝒳\mathcal X, we let ℳ(𝒳)\mathcal M(\mathcal X) be the set of all finite measures on 𝒳\mathcal X and Π(𝒳)\Pi(\mathcal X) be the set of probability measures on 𝒳\mathcal X. For μ∈ℳ(ℝd)\mu\in\mathcal M(\mathbb R^d), we denote its barycenter by bary⁡(μ):=∫ℝdxμ(dx)/μ(ℝd)\operatorname{bary}(\mu):=\int_{\mathbb R^d}x\mu(dx)/\mu(\mathbb R^d).
