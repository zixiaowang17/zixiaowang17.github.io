High-dimensional inference for dynamic treatment effects

Section 1 — dynamic treatment effect, equation (1.1)

Our parameter of interest is the dynamic treatment effect (DTE) between two treatment paths, aa and a′a', which is defined as follows: θ:=E[Y(a)]−E[Y(a′)]=θa−θa′,with θa:=E[Y(a)]. \theta:=E[Y(a)]-E[Y(a')]=\theta_a-\theta_{a'},\quad\text{with }\theta_a:=E[Y(a)]. (1.1)
