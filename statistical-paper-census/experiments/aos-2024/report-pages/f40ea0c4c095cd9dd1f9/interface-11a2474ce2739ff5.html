Section 2.1 — Change-point model

Change-point model. For each t∈ℕt\in\mathbb N, we assume that the conditional probability of the change happening at time tt given the past observations and the event {Lt−1=0}\{L_{t-1}=0\} is a function of only the first tt assigned treatments, i.e., there exists a function πt:[K]t→[0,1)\pi_t:[K]^t\to[0,1) such that Πt=πt(X1,…,Xt). \Pi_t=\pi_t(X_1,\ldots,X_t). Thus, the change-point model is determined by the prior probability π0∈[0,1)\pi_0\in[0,1) and the sequence of functions {πt,t∈ℕ}\{\pi_t,t\in\mathbb N\}. To emphasize that the distribution of the change-point, Θ\Theta, depends on the treatment assignment rule, 𝒳≡{Xt,t∈ℕ}\mathcal X\equiv\{X_t,t\in\mathbb N\}, we denote it by Θ𝒳\Theta_{\mathcal X}.
