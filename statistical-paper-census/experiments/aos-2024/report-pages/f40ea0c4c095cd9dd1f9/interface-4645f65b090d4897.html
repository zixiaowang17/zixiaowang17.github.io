Definition 2.4

Polynomials p1,…,pk:ℝd→ℝp_1,\ldots,p_k:\mathbb{R}^d\to\mathbb{R} are algebraically independent (over ℝ\mathbb{R}) if there is no nonzero polynomial q:ℝk→ℝq:\mathbb{R}^k\to\mathbb{R} such that q(p1(θ),…,pk(θ))q(p_1(\theta),\ldots,p_k(\theta)) is identically 00 for all θ∈ℝd\theta\in\mathbb{R}^d.

For any 𝒜⊆ℛ𝖦\mathcal{A}\subseteq \mathcal{R}^\mathsf{G}, its transcendence degree trdeg⁡(𝒜)\mathop{\mathrm{trdeg}}(\mathcal{A}) is the maximum cardinality of any algebraically independent subset A⊆𝒜A \subseteq \mathcal{A}. Any maximal such subset A⊆𝒜A \subseteq \mathcal{A} is a transcendence basis for 𝒜\mathcal{A}.
