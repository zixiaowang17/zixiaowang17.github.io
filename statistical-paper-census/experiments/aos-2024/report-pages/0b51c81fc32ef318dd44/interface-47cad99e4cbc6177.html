Section 1.1, sequentially interactive channels, equation (1)

We have i.i.d. data X1,…,XnX_1,\ldots,X_n drawn according to a distribution PP on a space 𝒳\mathcal{X}. Instead of observing the original sample {Xi}\{X_i\}, however, the statistician or learner sees only privatized data {Zi}\{Z_i\}, where ZiZ_i is drawn from a Markov kernel Q(⋅∣Xi)Q(\cdot\mid X_i) conditional on XiX_i (following information-theory, we call QQ the privacy channel [15]). We allow the channel to be sequentially interactive [25], meaning that ZiZ_i may depend on the previous (private) observations Z1,…,Zi−1Z_1,\ldots,Z_{i-1}, i.e. Zi∣Xi=x,Z1,…,Zi−1∼Q(⋅∣x,Z1:i−1). Z_i\mid X_i=x,Z_1,\ldots,Z_{i-1}\sim Q(\cdot\mid x,Z_{1:i-1}).
