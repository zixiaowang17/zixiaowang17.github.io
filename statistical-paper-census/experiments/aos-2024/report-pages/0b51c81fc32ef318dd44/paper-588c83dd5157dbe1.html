  • Transfer exponentSmall adaptation

    The finite-exponent condition is expressible, but least-exponent attainment and the source’s infinite-exponent convention remain unspecified.

  • Upper bound on the number of pullsSmall adaptation

    The known-parameter radius predicate and a proof that it reaches the stated threshold remain to be supplied for the Nat.find construction.
