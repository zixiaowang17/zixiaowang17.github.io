  • BandwidthSmall adaptation

    mathlib supplies asymptotic relations and limits, but the bandwidth wrapper needs the source’s simeq and integer-size conventions while preserving both conditions at the shared regime boundary.

    mathlib supplies operator-norm bounds and Fréchet derivatives, but the Schatten path spaces, the source’s mixed operator domains and the integrated uniform derivative bounds still require definitions and domain clarification.

    mathlib supplies conditional expectations and moment norms, but the dependence coefficient must retain the printed absolute-index and supremum order, with integrability supplied where the source’s p>0 wording is insufficient.

  • Equidistant pointsSmall adaptation

    mathlib supplies affine interpolation and limits, but the trimmed-grid wrapper must encode the paper’s indexing, distinct endpoints and asymptotic rate conditions.

  • Holomorphic functional calculusNew infrastructure

    mathlib supplies continuous functional calculus, but the paper’s Riesz–Dunford identification, Schatten-valued field mapping and eta-scaled spectral-domain conditions remain unestablished.

  • Linear mapsNew infrastructure

    mathlib supplies Bochner integrals and finite averages, but the Schatten-valued target and representative or regularity needed for point evaluation on the printed L1 domain remain to be supplied.

  • Mixing condition exhibiting polynomial decaySmall adaptation

    mathlib supplies big-O bounds and the conditional moment terms, but the wrapper must use the paper’s coordinate-summed dependence tail with 0<rho<1 and its established summability.

  • Sequential estimatorNew infrastructure

    mathlib supplies rank-one operators, floors and phase factors, but the exact sequential estimator and its source-qualified Schatten path, continuity and positivity claims remain to be developed.

  • Smoothness conditionsSmall adaptation

    mathlib supplies Fréchet derivatives and moment norms, but the trace-class smoothness wrapper must retain weighted coordinate summability and the stated almost-everywhere, per-frequency bound.

  • SpectrumSmall adaptation

    mathlib supplies operator spectra, but the Schatten field’s pointwise representative, uniformly separated spectral domain and the source’s L1-versus-L convention still need to be specified.

  • Time-varying spectral density operatorsNew infrastructure

    mathlib supplies rank-one operators and Bochner integration, but the trace-class Fourier-series construction and its stated regularity and positivity need development without inserting an unstated centering assumption.

  • Weight functionSmall adaptation

    mathlib supplies evenness, limits and big-O bounds, but the window predicate must combine them with the printed support and piecewise-continuity conditions without adding positive definiteness.

  • Locally stationary function-valued processSmall adaptation

    mathlib supplies moment distances and laws, but the locally stationary triangular-array wrapper must combine stationary finite-dimensional laws with both uniform approximation bounds and the source’s p-domain convention.

  • RepresentationsSmall adaptation

    mathlib supplies measurable maps, joint independence, laws and generated sigma-algebras, but the causal-history and independent-replacement wrapper must retain the source’s absolute-index convention.

  • WeightsSmall adaptation

    mathlib supplies the complex exponential and scalar operations, but the lag-window wrapper must retain the printed positive phase, normalization and bandwidth-dependent window.
