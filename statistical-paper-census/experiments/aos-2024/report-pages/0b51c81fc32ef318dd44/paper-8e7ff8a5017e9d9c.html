Theorem 3.3 (Lp convergence of a numerical scheme)

7 required APIs

Let Assumptions (A1) and (A2) hold, and let 𝐗̃tk\widetilde{\mathbf X}_{t_k} be a numerical approximation of the solution 𝐗tk\mathbf X_{t_k} of (1) at time tkt_k. If:

  1. The one-step approximation 𝐗̃tk=Φ̃h(𝐗̃tk−1)\widetilde{\mathbf X}_{t_k}=\widetilde\Phi_h(\widetilde{\mathbf X}_{t_{k-1}}) is LpL^p consistent of order q2−1/2q_2-1/2; and

  2. 𝐗̃\widetilde{\mathbf X} has bounded moments,

then 𝐗̃\widetilde{\mathbf X} is LpL^p convergent, p≥1p\geq1, of order q2−1/2q_2-1/2, that is, for k=1,…,Nk=1,\ldots,N, it holds: (𝔼[∥𝐗tk−𝐗̃tk∥2p])12p=R(hq2−1/2,𝐱0). \left(\mathbb E[\|\mathbf X_{t_k}-\widetilde{\mathbf X}_{t_k}\|^{2p}]\right)^{\frac1{2p}}=R(h^{q_2-1/2},\mathbf x_0).
