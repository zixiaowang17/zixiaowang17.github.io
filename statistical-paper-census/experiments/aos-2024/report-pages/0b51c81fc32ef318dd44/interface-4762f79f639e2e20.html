Section 5.1 — Functional differentiability

Let ℱ\mathcal F denote the class of all distributions on (ΩX,ℱX)(\Omega_X,\mathcal F_X). Let ℋ\mathcal H be a generic Hilbert space. Let T:ℱ→ℋT:\mathcal F\to\mathcal H be a mapping — such mappings are known as statistical functionals. Endow ℱ\mathcal F with the uniform metric, and ℋ\mathcal H with the metric induced by its inner product. Let F0F_0 be a member of ℱ\mathcal F.
