Theorem 9 (Adaptation to large signals, summary in Gaussian case)

The inherited model is explicitly specialized to Gaussian noise. This specifies the density family and independent noise in the sequence model.

Section 1.2 — sparse sequence model (1)

Consider the setting of Theorem 8 with ζ=2\zeta=2 (Gaussian noise). The following points consider the adaptation problem over the class Θ(r,β)\Theta(r,\beta) for some β∈(0,1)\beta\in(0,1) and β<r\beta<r. The results hold for the ℜ\mathfrak R–risk as well as for the normalised classification risk EθLC(θ,φ)/n1−βE_\theta\mathrm L_{\mathrm C}(\theta,\varphi)/n^{1-\beta}.

  1. Simultaneous adaptation to (r,β)(r,\beta) is impossible over the full range of parameters.

  2. Known exact sparsity sns_n, unknown rr: the top–sns_n procedure provides adaptation to rr.

  3. Unknown β\beta, known rr: a plug-in procedure provides adaptation to β\beta.

  4. Simultaneous adaptation to (r,β)(r,\beta) is possible in the regime κ(r,β,2)<1−β\kappa(r,\beta,2)<1-\beta.

Moreover, for point (i) the loss due to adaptation is polynomial in nn.
