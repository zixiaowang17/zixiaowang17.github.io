Heavy tailed series priors. Depending on the setting, we construct priors in L2:=L2[0,1]L^2:=L^2[0,1] via series expansions in either an orthonormal basis {φk:k≥1}\{\varphi_k:k\ge1\} or an orthonormal boundary-corrected wavelet basis {ψlk:l≥0,k∈𝒦l}\{\psi_{lk}:l\ge0,\ k\in\mathcal K_l\} where 𝒦l={0,…,2l−1}\mathcal K_l=\{0,\ldots,2^l-1\} and where we denote the scaling function as the first wavelet ψ00\psi_{00}. Without loss of generality we have taken the coarsest scale to be 1, while it is straightforward to accommodate for coarsest scales finer than 1. For more details, see [40, Section 4.3].
