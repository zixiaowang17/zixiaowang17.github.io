APIs required by Theorem 1 (General minimax upper bound) 12 required APIs
  • FunctionalSmall adaptation

    The functional still needs invertible noise covariance, a nonempty strict-positive matrix domain and the correctly ordered expected inverse with its trace budget.

  • Minimax riskSmall adaptation

    The robust minimax risk still needs its observed-data experiment indexed by both parameter and admissible noise kernel and a justified restriction from randomized to deterministic estimators.

  • Conditional covarianceSmall adaptation

    The conditional covariance bound still needs finite moments and a justified passage from fixed-direction almost-everywhere bounds to any simultaneous matrix inequality.

  • Conditional probabilitySmall adaptation

    The conditional-noise model still needs the almost-everywhere zero-mean and directional second-moment constraints on its probability kernel.

  • Conditionally centeredSmall adaptation

    Conditional mean zero still requires an integrable noise vector under almost every conditional law.

  • ConditionsSmall adaptation

    The admissible-noise class still needs the conjunction of the precise conditional mean and covariance-bound conditions relative to P.

  • EllipseSmall adaptation

    The constraint set still needs the inverse-metric norm and faithful treatment of the source's radius notation and endpoint.

  • Joint lawSmall adaptation

    The specified joint design-noise law is directly represented by the measure composition-product of P and the Markov kernel nu.

  • Measurable functionsSmall adaptation

    The estimator domain still needs to be restricted to measurable functions of the observed matrix and response.

  • Probabilistic structureSmall adaptation

    The random-operator model still needs its measurable Xi-indexed matrix map and probability law, without imposing additional moment or rank restrictions.

  • Random linear observationsSmall adaptation

    The observation model still needs its measurable pushforward with only the operator and response exposed to the estimator.

  • Squared normsSmall adaptation

    The norm construction still needs the positive-definite quadratic-form bridge and the inverse constraint metric kept distinct from the error metric.
