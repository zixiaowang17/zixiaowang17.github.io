Introduction — Tensor-on-tensor regression (1)–(2)

This paper studies a general class of problems termed tensor-on-tensor regression, which aims to characterize the relationships between covariates and responses in the form of scalars, vectors, matrices, or high-order tensors: 𝒴i=⟨𝒜i,𝒳*⟩*+ℰi,i=1,…,n. \mathcal Y_i=\langle\mathcal A_i,\mathcal X^*\rangle_*+\mathcal E_i,\qquad i=1,\ldots,n. 𝒴i,ℰi∈ℝpd+1×⋯×pd+m\mathcal Y_i,\mathcal E_i\in\mathbb R^{p_{d+1}\times\cdots\times p_{d+m}} are both order-mm tensors and are observations and unknown noise, respectively. 𝒳*∈ℝp1×⋯×pd×pd+1×⋯×pd+m\mathcal X^*\in\mathbb R^{p_1\times\cdots\times p_d\times p_{d+1}\times\cdots\times p_{d+m}} is an order-(d+m)(d+m) tensor parameter of interest. ⟨⋅,⋅⟩*\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_* is the contracted tensor inner product defined as ⟨𝒜i,𝒳*⟩*∈ℝpd+1×⋯×pd+m\langle\mathcal A_i,\mathcal X^*\rangle_*\in\mathbb R^{p_{d+1}\times\cdots\times p_{d+m}}, (⟨𝒜i,𝒳*⟩*)[j1,…,jm]=∑kl=1,l=1,…,dpl𝒜i[k1,…,kd]𝒳[k1,…,kd,j1,…,jm]*. Throughout the paper, we consider dd and mm to be fixed constants. We also stack all responses and errors to 𝒴,ℰ∈ℝn×pd+1×⋯×pd+m\mathcal Y,\mathcal E\in\mathbb R^{n\times p_{d+1}\times\cdots\times p_{d+m}}, where 𝒴[i,:,…,:]=𝒴i\mathcal Y_{[i,:,\ldots,:]}=\mathcal Y_i and ℰ[i,:,…,:]=ℰi\mathcal E_{[i,:,\ldots,:]}=\mathcal E_i.
