We consider processes that admit representations of the form Xt,T=𝔾(t,T,𝔣t), X_{t,T}=\mathbb G(t,T,\mathfrak f_t), (3.1) where 𝔣t=(ϵt,ϵt−1,…)\mathfrak f_t=(\epsilon_t,\epsilon_{t-1},\ldots) for an i.i.d. sequence {ϵt∣t∈ℤ}\{\epsilon_t\mid t\in\mathbb Z\} of random elements taking values in some measurable space SS, and where 𝔾:ℤ×ℕ×S∞→ℋ\mathbb G:\mathbb Z\times\mathbb N\times S^\infty\to\mathcal H is a measurable function.
