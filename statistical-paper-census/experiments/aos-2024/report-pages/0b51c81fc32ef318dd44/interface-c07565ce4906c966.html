Section 1.6 — BH procedure description

First, probably the most widely used multiple testing procedure is the so-called Benjamini–Hochberg procedure, introduced in [9]. For some level α\alpha, it is given by φαBH=(𝟏{|Xi|≥t̂})1≤i≤n\varphi_\alpha^{BH}=(\mathbf1\{|X_i|\ge\widehat t\})_{1\le i\le n} where the threshold t̂=t̂(α)\widehat t=\widehat t(\alpha) is defined as a specific intersection point between the empirical upper-tail distribution function of the XiX_i’s and a quantile curve of the noise distributions (see Section S-7 for details).
